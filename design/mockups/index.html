Round 2 — style directions on Option A's layout

Same rows, same structure, same interactions in every demo — only look and feel changes. Each style has its own default accent color suited to its personality; open Settings (⚙) in any of them to change theme mode (dark/light/auto) or accent, plus a density toggle as a bonus.
